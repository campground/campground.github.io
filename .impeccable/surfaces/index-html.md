# Homepage brief: single-page, vector-illustrated marketing site

Mode: Persuade. Visual authority: DESIGN.md ("The Trail Map"), inherited unchanged. This is a refinement of `index.html` and `_layouts/default.html` inside the established world, not a new world.

## 1. Job and audience

Founders, product leads and ops leads arriving from a referral or search, skimming. In one scroll they must learn what Campground builds, see proof (clients), and reach one action: Start a project (mailto). Skim path, top to bottom: hero → services → expertise → how we work → clients → run + improve → manifesto/contact → footer. The page runs from day to night: a daylight hero panorama on Birch, light working sections, purple arriving as dusk in the timeline, the night band, a Dusk manifesto, and a night footer. Section order is unchanged; the arc is carried by ground color, illustration and theme, not by moving sections.

## 2. Outcome and proof

- Primary action: "Start a project" (same words, same button, in header, hero, night band, contact band, and the clients row). Pine everywhere on light ground; the tokens re-point it to Lantern in the night band and to the inverse (white fill, Dusk text) on the manifesto band.
- Proof: three named clients with logos (Parker Palm Springs, Ward Village, Gramercy Park Hotel), each with the user-supplied summary line "Web Application + CMS". Cleared claims stay as they are (two-week cadence, one-working-day reply, senior team, on-call).
- Product truth: six services, eight technologies, three working steps, all existing copy kept verbatim except where noted below.

**Illustration format rule.** The look is vector: flat, faceted, hard-edged, the two shipped SVGs are the reference. The file format is whatever produces that look at the best weight, decided per asset at build time, not by hand-authoring requirement:
- Generated or painted artwork ships as raster: WebP, quality about 80, at 2× the layout size (panoramas 3200×1200, flat lay 960×960), the flat lay with a real alpha channel. Budget: each panorama under 250 KB, the flat lay under 120 KB; check the edges on light and dark ground for halos. A single `<img>` per asset, no `<picture>` set unless a budget is missed.
- Artwork that is drawn as paths ships as SVG only when it stays under about 40 KB minified and needs no embedded raster; then it may keep the `--illo-*` variable hooks. A traced raster does not qualify.
- Every asset has intrinsic `width`/`height`, the hero eager with `fetchpriority="high"`, the rest `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"`. `magick` and `cwebp` are on this machine for conversion.

## 4. Scope and boundaries

- Files: `index.html`, `_layouts/default.html`, three new illustrations (hero campsite panorama, night-band campsite panorama, services flat lay; format per the rule above), three client logo SVGs under `assets/logos/clients/`, one small script for nav (inline or `assets/js/nav.js`).
- Untouched: tokens (`_tailwind/campground.css`), DESIGN.md rules, hero copy, night band copy, all existing copy, the two existing illustration files. DESIGN.md's Spec Panel component, the Faint Contour note (hero and night band), the Night Band component's tent line, the Dusk entry ("the shipped pages do not use it yet"), the One Band Rule (Pine → Dusk), the contact-band component (Pine → Dusk), and the Footer line (white → night) go stale with this change; the documenter updates them after the build, not before.
- Anti-goals: no hero spec panel, no Pine ground anywhere (Pine stays a control and a rule; the one drenched band is Dusk), no purple above the timeline, no rounded corners past 4px, no shadows, no gradients, no clip-art gear, no invented client claims, no scroll-triggered animation.

## 5. States and ranges

- Nav: idle, active section, hover (Pine), focus (2px outline), menu open/closed; reduced motion disables smooth scroll.
- Timeline: 3 steps now; the layout holds 3–5.
- Clients: 3 now; layout holds 3–4. Summary lines ≤ 90 characters; a logo that fails to trace cleanly falls back to an Arvo wordmark.
- Illustration `<img>`s have width/height set to avoid layout shift; hero scene eager, night scene and services flat lay lazy. Missing or slow image: the bands keep their flat surface color and the text is fully legible with no image at all. Hero text must pass AA against whatever part of the scene it can touch at any width; the design intent is that it touches none.

## 6. Constraints and open decisions

- Build path: comp-led, the recorded default in `.impeccable/config.json`, stands. The three illustrations are produced as plates from the approved comp under the Illustration format rule (WebP, not SVG); the comp gate's refusal of SVG illustration no longer conflicts with the brief.
- WCAG 2.1 AA: menu is keyboard-operable, `aria-expanded` on the trigger, markers not color-only (rule plus `aria-current`), heart has text.
- No open decisions. Clients headline "Good company." confirmed; illustration format resolved by the rule above; build path stays comp-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One trail map, read from morning to night. The page is a single column of full-bleed bands whose ground darkens as the visitor descends: Birch day, white and sunken working sections, purple dusk, night, Dusk manifesto, night footer. It refuses the agency default of a gray hero, a stock photo and three icon cards, and it refuses the two-column "text left, image right" band repeated down the page: illustrations are grounds the text stands on, not pictures beside it.

OWN-WORLD: Birch and white flat surfaces divided by 1px Line; Pine as the only control color on light ground; Moss uppercase Archivo Expanded labels naming every section and fact; Arvo regular display over Archivo body; 2px corners, no shadows, no gradients. Illustration is flat, faceted, hard-edged, in the palette of the section it sits in: daylight greens in the hero, transparent flat lay in the services panel, night purples in the night band. Purple enters as dusk: Dusk rail and nodes in the timeline, a Dusk drench with a Lantern Bright topo field for the manifesto, Night Ground and the Night lockup in the footer. With the words removed the page still reads as a topographic sheet: hairlines, labels, a horizon, a tent.

STORY: A founder skims and learns in one screen what Campground builds; the flat lay shows the gear (six services), the strip shows the tools, the timeline shows the three-step way of working, the clients row shows "Good company.", the night band promises someone is on watch, the manifesto says what the firm believes and asks for one thing: Start a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT (1440×900): a sticky white header, 88px, Digital lockup left, five links with a 2px Pine underline on the active one, Pine "Start a project" right. Below it the Birch hero band fills the rest of the viewport. Left 8 columns from the top: Moss label "Design + development + AI", the lowercase Arvo headline "software that holds up in the field" at 72px over three lines, the lead paragraph at 18px capped at 672px, then the Pine button and the arrow link on one row. No spec panel. Across the full width, anchored to the band's bottom edge and occupying the lower 40% of the band, the daylight campsite panorama: faceted twin peaks right of center, a treeline, a still lake reflecting the peaks, one tent at the left third, a thread of smoke; the sky is the flat Birch surface so every word sits on plain ground. The headline's baseline clears the panorama's highest peak by at least 48px.

FORM: Trail-map single page with illustrated grounds, first and only candidate on the list: the surface was shaped from a precise brief and inherits the established world, so no concept roll ran and there is no seed key (new-work §3, extend an existing surface). Signature interaction: the header's Pine marker fading in under the active link as sections pass under it (Services marked while the hero is in view), and the disclosure menu on phones. Motion grammar: none beyond the marker's opacity transition and the menu; reduced motion removes the transition.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
