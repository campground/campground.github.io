What we do

Pack everything you need.

Strategy + discovery
Research, product definition and a roadmap you can budget against. We find the shortest path from idea to something people can use.
Web + mobile apps
Design and engineering, built to last. Rails, TypeScript and React on the web; React Native for iOS and Android; Elixir where uptime and scale matter most.
AI automation
Practical AI inside your product and your operations: workflows that run themselves, and measurement to prove it.

How we work

Small teams, senior people.

You work directly with the people building your product: no handoffs, no account layer.

  1. 01

    Discovery sprint

    Every engagement starts short. You know the scope, the cost and the first milestone before you commit to more.

  2. 02

    Two-week increments

    We ship in increments you can see and use, not status decks. Change course whenever the work tells you to.

  3. 03

    Launch, then stay

    No handoff to a support desk. The people who built it stay on it, and the next release is already on the map.
