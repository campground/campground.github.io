# Design System: Campground

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Trail Map"**

Campground's site is drawn like a survey map of good country: a faint contour ground, ruled edges, every fact under a label, routes numbered in order. The map is where the brand's two halves meet. The outdoors supplies the warmth (Pine and Moss greens, a lantern-lit night band, a backpack and a tent), and the cartography supplies the rigor (hairlines, a strict type scale, squared corners, nothing floating). A prospective client skimming the page should be able to read it the way they would read a trailhead sign: what is here, how far, which way.

Components are **sturdy and plainspoken**. They are built like gear: squared, legible, full-weight fills, strong outlines where an outline is needed, no ornament. Warmth comes from the copy, the color, and the two illustrations, never from soft shapes or decorative effects. The system is flat by construction. It rejects the stock Tailwind UI look (default indigo, default grays, unmodified marketing blocks), gradient washes, glassmorphism, and literal summer-camp pastiche such as clip-art tents or faux-wood texture.

The tokens are generated by the Campground Design System into `_tailwind/campground.css`; that file is the source of every value below and is not hand-edited. This document describes how the shipped site uses them.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Flat surfaces separated by 1px lines; depth is a surface step, not a shadow.
- Slab-serif display (Arvo) over a grotesque body (Archivo), with wide uppercase labels (Archivo Expanded) naming everything.
- Green colourway by default; night is a per-section theme, not a user dark mode.
- Corners are 2px. Nothing is rounder than 4px.
- A topography contour field sits behind the hero and the night band, always under 10% opacity.
- 44px minimum target height on every control.

## Colors

A restrained light ground with one deep green doing the work, a second brighter green reserved for labels, one fully committed green band at the close, and one night band in periwinkle.

### Primary
- **Pine** (`pine`): the brand's deep green. Primary buttons, the top rule on step cards, link and focus color, nav hover. It is `brand-600` / `brand-deep` in the tokens.
- **Pine, pressed** (`pine-hover`): hover state for Pine fills, and the full-bleed ground of the contact band, where the whole section is the color.
- **Pine Tint** and **Pine Wash** (`pine-tint`, `pine-wash`): pale greens for tinted fills and the hover state of the inverse button.

### Secondary
- **Moss** (`moss`): the bright green, used only for uppercase labels and eyebrows on light surfaces. It was chosen over the logo's lighter green because that one fails AA on white.
- **Moss, inverse** (`moss-inverse`): Moss for dark grounds.

### Tertiary
- **Dusk** (`dusk`): the purple colourway's deep tone. The purple theme exists in the tokens (`data-theme="purple"`) and is the accent colourway; the shipped pages do not use it yet.
- **Lantern** (`lantern`) and **Lantern Bright** (`lantern-bright`): the night theme's periwinkles. Lantern fills the button inside a night band; Lantern Bright sets labels and the contour lines there.
- **Night Ground**, **Night Raised**, **Night Ink** (`night-ground`, `night-raised`, `night-ink`): the night band's surface, raised surface, and text.

### Neutral
- **Granite** (`granite`): body and heading ink. Taken from the logo's wordmark gray, so text and logo match.
- **Granite Muted** (`granite-muted`): supporting copy, descriptions, footer text.
- **Birch** (`birch`): the page ground, a slightly green off-white.
- **Birch Sunken** (`birch-sunken`): recessed areas.
- **White** (`white`): raised surfaces: header, footer, the spec panel, the services section.
- **Line** (`line`) and **Line Strong** (`line-strong`): the 1px hairline that divides everything, and the stronger outline for controls that need a visible edge.
- **Danger** and **Danger Tint** (`danger`, `danger-tint`): error text and its background.

### Named Rules
**The Moss Is For Labels Rule.** Moss appears on uppercase labels and nowhere else. It is never a fill, never body text, never a button.

**The Faint Contour Rule.** The topography field stays under 10% opacity (6% on light, 8% at night) so text reads against a flat surface. It marks a section as ground; it is not a texture to turn up.

**The One Band Rule.** Exactly one section per page is drenched in Pine, and it is the close. Everywhere else, Pine is a control or a rule, not a ground.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Arvo (with Rockwell, Georgia, serif)
**Body Font:** Archivo (with system-ui, sans-serif)
**Label Font:** Archivo Expanded Bold (with Archivo, system-ui, sans-serif)

**Character:** A rounded slab serif over a plain grotesque, echoing the logo's lowercase slab wordmark and its wide uppercase descriptor. Arvo is set at regular weight even at display sizes; it gets its presence from size, not boldness. All three faces are self-hosted from `assets/fonts/` under the OFL.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400, 56px/60px, -0.01em): page-level headlines. The homepage hero runs larger than the token (48px, 72px from `sm` up) using Tailwind's own scale.
- **Headline** (400, 40px/46px, -0.01em): section titles, one per section.
- **Title** (700, 22px/28px): step and card titles. The only place Arvo goes bold.
- **Body Large** (400, 18px/28px): section lead paragraphs, in Granite Muted. Kept to about `max-w-2xl` (672px).
- **Body** (400, 16px/24px): descriptions, list entries, links.
- **Body Small** (400, 14px/20px): nav links (semibold), footer.
- **Label Large** (700, 14px/20px, 0.12em, uppercase): section eyebrows.
- **Label** (700, 12px/16px, 0.12em, uppercase): fact labels, step numbers, button text.

### Named Rules
**The Label First Rule.** Every section and every fact is named by an uppercase Moss label before anything else is said: the label gives the kind, the Arvo line gives the thing. A heading with no label above it is missing its map key.

**The Lowercase Summit Rule.** The page's single top headline is set in lowercase with no period ("software that holds up in the field", "off the trail"), matching the lowercase wordmark. Section headlines below it use sentence case.

## Layout

One centered column, `max-w-7xl` (1280px), with a 24px gutter that widens to 32px from `lg` (1024px). Sections are full-bleed bands stacked edge to edge, each with 96px of vertical padding, 128px from `sm` (640px). Bands alternate Birch and White and are separated by a 1px Line; the night band and the contact band break the alternation on purpose.

Inside a band, content is either a 12-column grid split 8/4 (hero: headline left, spec panel right, bottoms aligned), an even two-column split with a 48px gap (illustration beside text), or a lead block capped at `max-w-2xl` above a three-column list from `md` (768px). Everything collapses to a single column below those breakpoints, and the nav links hide below `md`, leaving the logo and the primary button. Spacing is Tailwind's 4px scale with no custom steps. Lists are ruled rows with 16px of padding, not cards.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth is expressed by stepping the surface (Birch page, White raised, Birch Sunken recessed) and by 1px Line borders. No shipped element casts a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Raised** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(39, 42, 53, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(39, 42, 53, 0.08)`): the one shadow token the design system defines. Unused on the site so far; reserved for things that genuinely float over content, such as a menu or dialog.

### Named Rules
**The Lines Not Shadows Rule.** A panel is separated from its ground by a 1px Line and a surface step. If something in the page flow seems to need a shadow, it needs a border.

## Shapes

Squared. Controls take a 2px corner (`rounded-xs`); 4px (`rounded-sm`) is the ceiling, and panels, bands, and images take no radius at all. Borders are 1px in Line, with one deliberate exception: the 2px Pine rule across the top of each numbered step. Dividers are full-width horizontal rules between rows; there are no pills, circles, or clipped shapes. The logo's angular twin-peak mark sets the geometry: sharp, faceted, no curves.

### Named Rules
**The Two Pixel Rule.** 2px on controls, 4px at most, 0 on containers. The design system's own note on the scale is "use nothing rounder".

## Components

Sturdy and plainspoken: squared, legible, full-weight, unornamented.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** squared with a barely eased corner (2px), 44px minimum height, 24px horizontal padding (16 to 20px in the header).
- **Primary:** Pine fill, white text set in Label (Archivo Expanded, 12px, uppercase, 0.12em). The same button and the same words, "Start a project", appear in the header, the hero, and the night band.
- **Inverse:** White fill with Pine text, for use on the Pine contact band. Hovers to Pine Wash.
- **Hover / Focus:** hover steps Pine to its pressed tone with no transition. Focus is a 2px outline, offset 2px, in the button's own color (white on the Pine band).
- **Night:** inside a `data-theme="night"` band the same classes resolve to a Lantern fill with near-black text.

### Text Links
- **Style:** Body, semibold, Granite, with a trailing arrow (`→`), hovering to Pine. Used as the quiet second action beside a primary button.
- **On Pine:** white, underlined with a 4px offset.

### Spec Panel
- **Character:** the trail marker. A bordered White panel in the hero holding the facts a skimming buyer wants.
- **Structure:** a definition list; each row is a Moss label over a Body value, 20px by 24px padding, rows divided by a 1px Line. No radius, no shadow.

### Ruled List
- **Style:** a definition list of rows with 16px vertical padding, a 1px Line above each row and below the last. Label in Moss, description in Granite Muted. This is the default way to present a set of parallel items; it replaces the icon-card grid.

### Step Cards
- **Style:** an ordered list in three columns. Each step opens with a 2px Pine top rule, then a numeral label ("01"), a Title, and a Body paragraph. No box, no background.

### Navigation
- **Style:** White bar with a 1px Line beneath. Logo left (40px tall, 56px from `sm`), three Body Small semibold links in Granite hovering to Pine, primary button right. Links are hidden below `md`; the button stays.
- **Footer:** White, 1px Line above, icon mark with a one-line description left, email and copyright right.

### Night Band
- **Character:** a full section with `data-theme="night"`, which re-points every token: Night Ground surface, Night Ink text, Lantern controls, Lantern Bright labels and contour lines. It carries the "we're on watch" message and the tent illustration. It is a place in the page, not a dark-mode toggle.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use the design system's tokens (`brand-*`, `surface`, `ink`, `ink-muted`, `line`, `font-display`, `font-label`, `text-label`, `text-display-lg`) and rename `indigo-` to `brand-` on any pasted Tailwind UI markup, even though the alias makes it render on-brand.
- **Do** put an uppercase Moss label above every section title and every fact.
- **Do** separate surfaces with a 1px Line and a surface step.
- **Do** keep every control at least 44px tall with a visible 2px focus outline.
- **Do** set a night section with `data-theme="night"` on the section and let the tokens re-point; don't hand-pick dark colors.
- **Do** present parallel items as ruled rows or top-ruled steps.

### Don't:
- **Don't** round anything past 4px, or put a radius on a panel, band, or image.
- **Don't** add shadows to in-flow elements, and don't add gradients, glass, or blur anywhere.
- **Don't** use Moss for anything but labels, or the logo's light green (`#70aa43`) for text: it fails AA on white.
- **Don't** raise the contour field's opacity to 10% or beyond.
- **Don't** ship a block that reads as stock Tailwind UI: default indigo, default gray scale, a gray hero with three icon cards.
- **Don't** illustrate with clip-art camping props or faux-wood and canvas textures. The two flat illustrations and the contour field are the whole outdoor vocabulary.
- **Don't** hand-edit `_tailwind/campground.css`; change the tokens in the Campground Design System and re-export.
